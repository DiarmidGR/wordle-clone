Wordle

How to Play

Wordle is a daily word puzzle. Guess the word in 6 tries.

  • Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word.
  • The color of tiles will change to show how close your guess was.
W
W is in the word and in the correct spot.
P
P is in the word but in the wrong spot.
V
V is not in the word in any spot.

Hard mode (optional): every revealed hint must be used in the next guess.

Have fun!
